\begin{theorem}[Pythagoras][label=thm:pythagoras]
Let \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) be the sides of a right triangle. Then \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \).
\end{theorem}

As shown in \ref{thm:pythagoras}, the Pythagorean Theorem holds.
